pangenome navigator

Genes grouped by the position they hold, not the identity they share.

A pangenome tool built on sequence identity has a floor — Panaroo will not merge below 70 % amino-acid identity, Roary below 95 %. Genes that diverge past it become separate families however obviously they are the same thing, and those are not a random sample of the genome: they are the loci under diversifying selection — surface antigens, capsule biosynthesis, flagellin, fimbrial adhesins, anti-phage defence.

A Nuna locus is defined differently: a position in the genome that a genome must fill, whose occupants may share no detectable sequence homology at all. Whether that is better described as one locus with several alleles, or as several genes, is the open question — so each page shows the evidence rather than asserting the answer.

A research prototype. Each page is built from one clustering model over one probe cohort, and carries that model's own provenance and accessory-fidelity audit in its footer — including its residuals. Nothing on a page is re-derived for display. The model is named there, not here, so the two cannot drift apart.

Each page is one self-contained file of roughly 12 MB, so the first load is slow and everything after it is instant — there are no further requests. Best on a wide screen.
